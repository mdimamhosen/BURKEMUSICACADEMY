A few helpful notes

Good questions.
Good beginnings.

Still wondering where to start? We’ll help you sort through it together.

Ask about your goals ↗
What ages does BMA work with?

Early Starters welcomes children ages 2–5 with a parent or caregiver. BMA’s private music training serves children, teens, and adults.

Can students take lessons online?

Yes. BMA offers in-person and virtual instruction for students in voice, piano, strings, and musical theater.

What happens in a consultation?

It’s a focused conversation about your background, training, goals, and possible next steps at BMA.

Do students get performance opportunities?

Students are welcome at Spring and Winter recitals and can take part in workshops, studio classes, performance outreach, and competitions.
